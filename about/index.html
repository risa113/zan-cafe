The Culinary Soul of Zan Cafe

Where artisanal baking, single-origin roasts, and the vibrant flavors of Melapalayam converge in a sophisticated, welcoming ambiance.

Rooted in Melapalayam, Crafted for Discerning Palates

Nestled in Naniyar Nagar, Melapalayam, Tirunelveli, Zan Cafe is where culinary artistry meets warm southern hospitality. Rated 4.7 stars by our cherished guests, we specialize in handcrafted gourmet burgers, wood-fired pizzas, signature chillers, and specialty espresso coffees.

Our founders set out to build a cafe that broke away from generic commercial fast food. Every brioche bun is toasted in European butter; every patty is seasoned and smashed fresh to order; and every batch of chicken uses a proprietary blend of aromatic Melapalayam spices.
